# Symphony-of-Life-Generative-Art

A collection page for **Symphony of Life** — fifteen works drawn with geometric primitives in p5.js, presented as a collection with a works grid, surfaces, process, and commission sections.

**Live:** `https://reyrove.github.io/Symphony-of-Life-Generative-Art/`

---

## Overview

**Symphony of Life** is a collection of fifteen works, each drawn with a small vocabulary of geometric primitives — **circles, rectangles, triangles, quads, bézier curves, arcs, and lines** — and accompanied by a written story. The works range from illustrative (a crow beside a human, a dance studio, a fish named Azure) to interior landscape (a figure under a perpetual black sky, a woman watching a sunset) to controlled abstraction (a 100 × 100 seed-based grid, a spiral inside a hexagon, horizontal bands emerging from distortion).

The collection is held together by the idea of **life** — the small recurring forms that shape a life, and the stories that give those forms meaning. Some works literally depict living things (trees, fish, owls, blood cells); others are about the shapes life takes as it moves through time.

The page functions as a **collection and licensing surface** for fashion houses, textile studios, and surface designers. All fifteen works are available to license individually or as a set.

---

## What Makes This Collection Different

**Symphony of Life** sits alongside the artist's other collections (Polar Memories, Wandering Eyes, Fibonacci's Spin, and the generative catalogue) but has a distinct character:

| Collection | Format | Character |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Polar Memories** | Six mixed works | p5.Polar geometry, stills + GIFs |
| **Wandering Eyes** | Five mixed works | Geometric narrative, story-led |
| **Fibonacci's Spin** | Two fixed editions | Fibonacci orbit, animated GIFs |
| **Symphony of Life** | Fifteen mixed works | Geometric narrative + controlled abstraction |

Where **Wandering Eyes** focuses on the wandering gaze as a unifying gesture, **Symphony of Life** is broader — it is a small library of fables, each told in the same geometric language but with a different voice. Three of the fifteen works — **Matrix Rain 01**, **The Hextech Singularity**, and **VIOLET STRATA** — sit at the abstract end of the collection, contributing a more structural register to an otherwise narrative set.

## Features of the Collection Page

### Hero

A single square canvas displaying **Sunset Serenade** — the first animated work and the most representative of the collection's palette. Sunset colours, ocean motion, and the "Symphony" title tie it directly to the collection name.

### Works Grid

A **3-column grid** showing all fifteen works, each as a card with:

- A live preview (JPG or GIF) loaded from `/images/`
- The work title in Cormorant Garamond
- The story opening in DM Mono
- Two metadata rows: primitives, format

The 3 × 5 layout keeps the page compact while showing the full collection at a glance. On tablet the grid drops to 2 columns; on mobile to 1.

### Surfaces Section

Four representative works — **Sunset Serenade**, **Whispers of the World**, **Matrix Rain 01**, and **VIOLET STRATA** — are shown across four surfaces each:

- **Print** (1:1) — `background-size: contain`
- **Scarf** (3:1) — three repeats horizontally
- **Textile** (4:3) — 2×2 tile
- **Wall** (2:3) — two repeats vertically

The GIFs and JPGs are reused via CSS `background-image`, so the browser caches each file once and serves it to all tiles.

### Process Section

Three cards on the shared logic:

- **Geometric Vocabulary** — the small set of primitives
- **Each Work Has One** — the story-led approach
- **A Symphony of Forms** — the life motif that holds the collection together

### Commission Section

Collection-level licensing:

- **Collection licence** — all fifteen works together
- **Commission a work** — a new composition in the same vocabulary
- **Systems** — a private Symphony of Life tool for your studio

### Colophon

Standard footer with collection nav, works list (all fifteen), and studio contact.

## Customisation

### Change the cover work

The cover currently shows **Sunset Serenade**. To feature a different work, edit the `src` in the cover `<img>`:

```html
<div class="cover-art">
  <img src="images/sunset-serenade.gif" alt="..." loading="eager" />
</div>
```

### Change the surfaces shown

The surfaces section shows four of the fifteen works. To show different works, edit the `background-image` URLs in the `<div class="surface-work-head">` + `<div class="surface-grid">` blocks.

### Change the works grid layout

The works grid uses CSS grid with `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr)`. Change to `1fr 1fr` for a two-column layout or `1fr` for a single column. With fifteen works, three columns produces a balanced 3 × 5 grid.

### Replace the mockups

Swap the three PNGs in `images/` or update the `src` attributes in the Commission section.

### Add a sixteenth work

Add a new `<article class="work-card">` block to the works grid and update the collection counts in the cover meta, statement, and colophon. The grid will reflow — a sixteenth work would leave one cell empty on the last row.

### Change the story openings

Each work card carries the opening one to three sentences of the story. Edit the `.work-card-desc` text. The full stories live on the individual work pages (see below).

## Notes on the Source

This page does not use p5.js. The fifteen works were originally drawn as p5.js sketches using geometric primitives; the outputs were captured as JPGs (for the stills) and GIFs (for the animated works), and are presented here as images.

**Why images rather than live sketches:**

- Images are lighter than continuous canvas redraws — the browser handles playback natively.
- The image files are cached after first load, so the surface tiles reuse the same files.
- The page has no external dependency beyond Google Fonts.

**One trade-off:** the GIF's frame rate is locked into the file. The two animated sketches originally ran at their own rates; the GIFs run at whatever rate was encoded.
